One Root, Many Nouns

From the single verb كَتَبَ Arabic builds the writer, the written, the office, the act of writing… Each noun has its own pattern. Learn the twelve derived forms, build them yourself in the word factory, and find them all in the Qur’an.

The big idea

Doer & receiver

اسْمُ الفَاعِلِ وَاسْمُ المَفْعُولفَاعِلٌ · مَفْعُولٌ

فَاعِلThe doer. Add an alif after the first letter and kasra before the last: لَاعِبٌ، قَاتِلٌ.
مَفْعُولThe receiver. A مَـ in front and a و before the last letter: مَقْتُولٌ، مَلْعُوبٌ.

Intensive forms

صِيَغُ المُبَالَغَةWhen the doer does it a lot

The ideaA doer noun says someone does something; an intensive noun says they do it again and again, or to a great degree. A لَاعِبٌ plays; a لَعَّابٌ is always playing.

Place, time & tool

المَكَانُ وَالزَّمَانُ وَالآلَةمَفْعَل · مَفْعِل · مِفْعَال

Place & time — one patternThe place or time of an action is مَفْعَل when the present has fatḥa or ḍamma on the middle letter (يَلْعَبُ ← مَلْعَبٌ، يَقْتُلُ ← مَقْتَلٌ) and مَفْعِل when it has kasra (يَجْلِسُ ← مَجْلِسٌ). A few words are exceptions: مَشْرِقٌ، مَغْرِبٌ، مَسْجِدٌ take kasra.
مَلْعَبٌ
playground
مَقْتَلٌ
place of killing
مَشْرِقٌ
east, place of rising
مَغْرِبٌ
west, place of setting
ToolThe instrument that does the action, with a مِـ in front: مِفْتَاحٌ — the thing you open with (فَتَحَ). Common patterns are مِفْعَال، مِفْعَل، مِفْعَلَة.
From the Qur’an
رَّبُّ الْمَشْرِقِ وَالْمَغْرِبِ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ
place — Sūrah al-Muzzammil, 73:9: “Lord of the east and the west; there is no god but Him.” The deck’s own pair.
قَدْ عَلِمَ كُلُّ أُنَاسٍ مَّشْرَبَهُمْ
place of شَرِبَ — Sūrah al-Baqarah, 2:60: “each people knew its drinking place.”
إِلَى اللَّهِ مَرْجِعُكُمْ جَمِيعًا
place/time of رَجَعَ — Sūrah al-Mā’idah, 5:48: “to Allah is your return, all together.”
لَمَسْجِدٌ أُسِّسَ عَلَى التَّقْوَىٰ
place of سَجَدَ, an exception that takes kasra — Sūrah at-Tawbah, 9:108: “a mosque founded on piety.”
الْمِصْبَاحُ فِي زُجَاجَةٍ
tool, مِفْعَال — Sūrah an-Nūr, 24:35: “the lamp is in a glass.”
وَعِندَهُ مَفَاتِحُ الْغَيْبِ لَا يَعْلَمُهَا إِلَّا هُوَ
tool: the plural of the مِفْتَاح family — Sūrah al-An‘ām, 6:59: “and with Him are the keys of the unseen.”
Action nouns

المَصْدَرُ وَالمَرَّةُ وَالهَيْئَةThe action itself, once, and the way

Three nouns from one action المَصْدَر is the action in general: قَتْلًا، ضَرْبًا — it has no single pattern, so learn it with the verb.
اسْمُ المَرَّة (فَعْلَة) is one occurrence: قَتْلَةٌ، لَعْبَةٌ.
اسْمُ الهَيْئَة (فِعْلَة) is the manner: قِتْلَةٌ، مِشْيَةٌ.

Quality & comparison

الصِّفَةُ المُشَبَّهَةُ وَاسْمُ التَّفْضِيلBeing, and being more

Lasting quality — الصِّفَةُ المُشَبَّهَةThe doer noun describes an action (كَاتِبٌ, writing now). A lasting-quality adjective describes what someone is: شُجَاعٌ (brave), عَطْشَانُ (thirsty). Common patterns: فَعِيل، فَعْلَان، أَفْعَل، فُعَال.
Comparative — اسْمُ التَّفْضِيلPattern أَفْعَل: أَكْبَرُ (greater), أَحْسَنُ (better). The two shortened forms خَيْرٌ (better) and شَرٌّ (worse) are the ones in your deck.
From the Qur’an
يَحْسَبُهُ الظَّمْآنُ مَاءً
lasting quality, فَعْلَان like عَطْشَان — Sūrah an-Nūr, 24:39: “the thirsty one thinks it is water.”
رَجَعَ مُوسَىٰ إِلَىٰ قَوْمِهِ غَضْبَانَ أَسِفًا
lasting quality, فَعْلَان — Sūrah al-A‘rāf, 7:150: “Moses returned to his people, angry and grieved.” (A diptote, so no tanwīn.)
وَالْآخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ وَأَبْقَىٰ
comparative — Sūrah al-A‘lā, 87:17: “while the Hereafter is better and more lasting.”
إِنَّ شَرَّ الدَّوَابِّ عِندَ اللَّهِ الصُّمُّ الْبُكْمُ
comparative — Sūrah al-Anfāl, 8:22: “the worst of living creatures in Allah’s sight are the deaf and dumb.”
وَلَذِكْرُ اللَّهِ أَكْبَرُ
comparative, أَفْعَل — Sūrah al-‘Ankabūt, 29:45: “and the remembrance of Allah is greater.”
